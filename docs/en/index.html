Two temperatures,
graphed on your phone.

Connect to an FEG2 thermometer over Bluetooth Low Energy and record both channels once per second.
Save records on your device, look back at them, or overlay one as a reference for your next run.

Read the guide GitHub

Features

Two channels, live

Temperature 1 (pink) and temperature 2 (blue) are shown in large type with the elapsed time, and drawn on the graph as they come in.

Save and review

Save records of up to 30 minutes on your device, and organize them with a title and notes.

Use a past run as a reference

A bookmarked record is drawn faintly behind the live graph, so you can see how this run differs from the last.

CSV export

Save the per-second temperatures as a CSV file that opens directly in Excel and other spreadsheets.

Adjust the device from the app

Change the brightness of the 7-segment display and calibrate each temperature from the app.

Your data stays on the device

The app does not use the internet. All records are stored on your device.

Requirements

  • A phone or tablet running Android 8.0 or later with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
  • An FEG2 thermometer (a device that sends temperatures over BLE)
