Guide

FEG2 / Android 8.0 or later

The basic flow: connect with the Bluetooth button → start recording with ▶ → stop with ⏸ and press Save → review it in the LOG tab.

1. Getting started (permissions and Bluetooth)

  1. Open the app and allow the permissionsOn first launch the app asks for permissions such as Nearby devices and Notifications. They are needed to find and connect to the device.
  2. Turn Bluetooth onThe connect button is disabled while Bluetooth is off.
  3. (Android 11 and earlier only) Turn Location onOn Android 11 and earlier, Android requires Location (GPS) to be on to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app does not use your location.

If you denied a permission, you can ask for it again later from SETTING → Permissions → Request permissions.

2. Connect the device

  1. Turn on the FEG2 thermometer
  2. In the CHART tab, press the “+” button at the bottom leftThe control buttons (Bluetooth, ▶, Save, Reset) appear above it.
  3. Press the Bluetooth buttonThe icon changes while the app searches, and it connects automatically when it finds the device. Once connected, the temperatures appear at the top right.
The app remembers your device

The first device you connect to is remembered, and from then on the app connects only to that device. To switch to another device, long-press the BLE device row in the SETTING tab to forget it.

Pressing the Bluetooth button while connected disconnects. Pressing it while searching stops the search.

3. Record temperatures

  1. Press ▶ to start recordingThe stopwatch starts and both temperatures are added to the graph once per second.
  2. Press ⏸ to stopPress ▶ again to continue from where you stopped.
  3. Press Save (↓) to saveAvailable only while stopped. “Saving” is shown, and the record is added to the LOG tab when it finishes.
  4. Press Reset (↻) to start overWhile stopped, this clears the time and the graph. Unsaved data is cleared too.
Records are limited to 30 minutes

After 30 minutes recording stops automatically and the record so far is saved.

Recording continues when you close the app or switch to another app, and the elapsed time and temperatures are shown in the notification. Pressing “Stop” in the notification disconnects the device and ends the recording. Unsaved data is lost when you do this.

4. Reading the CHART screen

WhereWhat it shows
Top rightElapsed time (gray), temperature 1 (pink), temperature 2 (blue)
Left edgeTemperature scale (the range can be changed in SETTING)
BottomTime scale. The graph scrolls horizontally
Faint lines on the graphThe bookmarked record (reference), set in LOG

Tap an empty part of the screen to hide the tabs and buttons and see more of the graph. Tap again to bring them back.

5. Review records (LOG)

Saved records are listed as cards in the LOG tab. Tap a card to open its graph. The buttons on the right do the following.

ButtonWhat it does
Delete (trash)Deletes the record. It is deleted immediately, without confirmation.
BookmarkMakes this record the reference, drawn faintly on the CHART graph. Its card in the list shows a colored bookmark
Edit (pencil)Add a title and a description (up to 3 lines)
ExportSaves the record as a CSV file in a location you choose

To remove the bookmark, long-press the colored bookmark on the card in the list.

CSV format

One row per second. The file is UTF-8 with a BOM, so it opens in Excel without garbled text. The header row is in Japanese:

経過秒,時間,温度F(℃),温度S(℃)
0,00:00,25.3,24.8
1,00:01,25.4,24.9

The columns are elapsed seconds, time (mm:ss), temperature 1 (°C) and temperature 2 (°C).

6. Settings (SETTING)

The settings screen is in Japanese. The items, from top to bottom:

ItemDescription
Graph temperature rangeThe temperature range shown on the graph (0–500 °C, in 10 °C steps). Default is 0–230 °C
ThemeSystem (Android 10 and later), Dark, Light
BLE deviceThe address of the remembered device, and the name and firmware version of the connected device. Long-press the address row to forget it
Device statusWhether Bluetooth (and Location on Android 11 and earlier) is on
7-segment brightnessBrightness of the thermometer display (0–8). Can be changed only while connected
Temperature 1 / 2 calibrationCorrects an offset in the measured temperature. Enter a value from -5.0 to 5.0 and press the send button. Can be sent only while connected
PermissionsAsks again for any permissions that were not granted

7. Troubleshooting

The Bluetooth button is disabled
  • Check that Bluetooth is on.
  • On Android 11 and earlier, turn Location (GPS) on as well.
  • The Device status section in SETTING shows which one is off.
The device is not found or does not connect
  • Check that the FEG2 thermometer is on and close to the phone.
  • Check that the Nearby devices permission is allowed. You can ask for it again from SETTING → Permissions.
  • If you connected to a different thermometer before, the app only searches for that one. Long-press the BLE device row in SETTING to forget it.
The Save or Reset button is disabled

Both are available only while recording is stopped with ⏸. Save is available only when there is an unsaved record.

The temperature is a little off

Use Calibration in SETTING to correct each temperature by up to ±5.0 °C.

The graph goes off the screen

Widen the graph temperature range in SETTING.

No notification appears

Android 13 and later require the notification permission. Turn it on in the system Settings → Apps → FEG2 → Notifications.

If this doesn't help, let us know in GitHub Issues.
