Privacy Policy

FEG2 / Effective and last updated: October 17, 2026

This app does not collect any personal information. Recorded temperature data is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or to any server. The app does not have permission to access the internet.

This privacy policy explains how the Android app “FEG2” (the “App”), provided by bunnmei (the “Developer”), handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The Developer does not collect any user information through the App. Specifically:

  • There is no account registration or sign-in.
  • The App does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or similar information.
  • The App does not use any tools (SDKs) that send data elsewhere, such as advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.
  • The App does not connect to the internet, and the Developer does not operate any servers.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Temperature records: records you save (per-second temperatures, the date and time recorded, and the title and description you add).
  • Settings: graph temperature range, theme, bookmarked record, device brightness and calibration values, and the Bluetooth address of the last thermometer you connected.

This data is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere by the App. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

If you use CSV export, the file is saved to the location you choose. What happens to it after that (for example, sharing it) is up to you.

If Android backup (such as backup to your Google Account) is enabled on your device, Android may include the data above in that backup. This is performed by Google's service according to your device settings.

3. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhy it is used
Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect)To find and connect to the FEG2 thermometer and receive temperatures. It is not used to determine your location
Location (Android 11 and earlier only)On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires this permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. The App does not obtain or store your location
NotificationsTo show the elapsed time and temperatures in the notification while recording
Foreground serviceTo keep recording when the App is closed

Information obtained through these permissions is used on your device only for the purposes above, and is never sent to the Developer or third parties.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the Developer holds no user information, none is shared with third parties.

If you obtained the App from Google Play, Google handles information such as downloads under its own privacy policy. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

5. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, for example when the App's features change. When it does, this page will be updated along with the “last updated” date above.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use GitHub Issues.

The App's source code is available on GitHub.
